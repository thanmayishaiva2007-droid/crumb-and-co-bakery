HOW IT ALL BEGAN

From a little kitchen to a world of sweetness.

Crumb & Co. was born from a simple belief: the best memories are often made around something delicious.

What started as a passion for baking slowly became a dream of creating a place where every cake, pastry and loaf could bring a little happiness into someone's day.

We wanted to create more than a bakery. We wanted to create an experience filled with warmth, beautiful flavours and thoughtful little details.

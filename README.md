# 🍰 Crumb & Co. — The Art of Baking

Welcome to **Crumb & Co. — The Art of Baking**, an interactive bakery website designed to bring the warmth of a bakery experience into a simple and user-friendly web interface.

The website allows customers to explore baked products, add items to a shopping cart, place orders, request customized cakes, view previous orders, and track their delivery status.

## 🌟 Features

* 🧁 Browse bakery products by category
* 🔎 Search for products
* 🛒 Add products to a shopping cart
* ➕➖ Increase or decrease product quantities
* 📦 Stock availability and out-of-stock management
* 🎂 Custom cake order form
* 📋 Order history
* 🚚 Order delivery tracking
* 🔐 Login page
* 👨‍💼 Admin order management
* 🎉 Festive offer countdown
* 📱 Responsive and user-friendly interface
* 💾 Local storage based order and cart management

## 🛠️ Technologies Used

* **HTML5** — Website structure
* **CSS3** — Styling and responsive design
* **JavaScript** — Interactivity and functionality
* **LocalStorage** — Cart, orders, stock and custom-order data management

## 🛍️ Main Website Sections

### 🏠 Home

Introduces Crumb & Co. and highlights bakery offerings, festive offers and important website features.

### 🧁 Menu

Displays available bakery products with categories, prices, descriptions, stock availability and shopping-cart functionality.

### 🎂 Custom Cakes

Allows customers to submit their requirements for a customized cake, including occasion, flavour, size, design and delivery details.

### 📋 My Orders

Allows customers to view their previously placed orders.

### 🚚 Delivery Tracking

Customers can enter their Order ID and view the current order status and delivery information.

### 👨‍💼 Admin

Provides a simple interface for managing order statuses such as:

* Order Placed
* Preparing
* Dispatched
* Delivered
* Delivery Failed

## 💡 Project Highlights

Crumb & Co. was developed as part of an **on-the-spot Web Wizards competition**.

The competition consisted of **three rounds**:

**Round 1 — Dashboard Creation**
We created a functional dashboard on the spot based on the given requirements.

**Round 2 — Website Development**
We developed the Crumb & Co. bakery website on the spot, implementing multiple interactive features.

**Round 3 — Modification & Enhancement**
We were given additional requirements and had to modify and enhance the existing website within the given time while maintaining its functionality.

Our team successfully completed all three rounds and secured **2nd Prize (Runner-Up)** in the competition. 🥈
